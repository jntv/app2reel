## Purpose and users

Help a product owner turn a product name, description, purpose, URL, actors and features into a screen-based demo and a promotional motion film. Today this generally requires manual research, recording, editing, and motion design. The app serves product founders, marketers, and demo creators running a local copy on their own laptop. A product test account is distinct from the ChatGPT account used for AI.

## Constraints and open decisions

Local single-user execution; one heavy job at a time; up to 20 scenes/180 seconds per film at 720p/1080p. The AI planner accepts walkthrough targets of 10–180 seconds and promo targets of 10–90 seconds, with independent scripts and clocks. Codex CLI, Chromium and local FFmpeg are required. Screenshots/page text are processed by OpenAI; media rendering is local. Default read-only request policy can prevent POST-based data loading. Complex login, canvas apps, inaccessible routes, and role-specific permissions may require manual intervention. Saved product sessions expire after eight hours and are lost when the server stops. Optional designed adult Indian-English Qwen voices currently require Apple Silicon macOS 14+; their fixed synthetic references and local setup are documented in README. Production use still needs target-product validation and creative review. Future direct SIWC OAuth, multi-role journeys, asset import, higher-quality capture, and arbitrary CGI are undecided extensions.
