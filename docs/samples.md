# Sample films

[Watch in the README](../README.md#watch-the-sample-films) · [Install App2Reel](installation.md) · [Voice setup](voices.md)

These are complete App2Reel films made for SmartSevak, using a recorded product tour and reviewed scripts and edits. Turn sound on in the player to hear narration and music.

## Product walkthrough

An 88-second landscape film with real browser footage, eased camera moves, pointer highlights, focus outlines, and word-highlighted subtitles. The light-gray rectangles are intentional privacy masks over account and personal details, including during camera moves and transitions.

Some saved masks cover broad table regions and remain visible on empty views. Masks belong to this sample's edit; you can [adjust per-scene privacy masks](usage.md#privacy-masks) for your own recording.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f9e535a2-9986-466b-a1a9-bbdaa06710e0

## Launch film

A 55-second portrait film with animated product-inspired interface models, kinetic headlines, layered motion, and branded opening/closing cards. Its interfaces are drawn from the product's design; they do not display the private recording or screenshots.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f42d8c2f-7ad2-4305-9469-382d8cadd630

## Settings used

| Setting | Walkthrough | Launch film |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duration | 88 seconds | 55 seconds |
| Public playback resolution | 1280 × 720 | 1080 × 1920 |
| Frame rate | 30 fps | 30 fps |
| Movement | Cinematic | Cinematic |
| Narration | Designed Indian English · adult woman | Same voice |
| Subtitles | Burned-in active-word highlights | Burned-in active-word highlights |
| Music | Original instrumental bed with speech ducking | Same arrangement system |
| Branding | Product-themed intro and outro | Product-themed intro and outro |

The selected voice uses the optional Qwen model pack, which currently requires Apple Silicon/macOS 14 or newer. It has a larger one-time download than the default Indian-English Melo voice. Preview your chosen voice before rendering; see [voice choices and platform requirements](voices.md).

The public walkthrough reuses the reviewed narration take and timing. The privacy edit does not change its script or duration. The portrait film is the existing finished export.
